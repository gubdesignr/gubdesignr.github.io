About us

Engineering Amplifiers for Home, Stage, and Studio.

PhonoVox

With 30 years of experience across the audio industry in both sales and product development, we have worked with British audio icons such as Naim and Bowers & Wilkins, championing ground-breaking designs like the Zeppelin, helping bring it to prominence in the mid-2000s.
We have thus gained a deep understanding of emerging trends and customers’ audio experience. As audiophiles ourselves, we appreciate and value quality engineering, which makes it an honour to be working with Lint Audio UK.

Lint Audio UK

Lint Audio has been manufacturing valve audio for the past 4 years in South Africa. Over three decades of experience in aircraft avionics and military design has allowed us to produce a range of competitive products.
With a focus on well-engineered, robust processes, valve audio need not be expensive, selecting valve types and materials that are easily sourced.
Our amplifier range is designed to be built in stages. Start with a pre amp. This may be used on existing solid state equipment. Then by internal bridges, can be adjusted accordingly.
Visit https://lintaudio.com/ for more information.
